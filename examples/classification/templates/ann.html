First hidden layer maps (per-neuron) Color shows:
How to read these mini-maps (super simple):
  • Each picture is one tiny "rule checker" (a neuron).
  • Colors show how much it likes a point: red = strong yes, blue = no, white = in‑between.
  • The pale line between red and blue is the neuron's border (where it is unsure, z ≈ 0).
  • ReLU: only the red side is on; the blue side is off.
  • Tanh/Sigmoid: smooth change from blue → white → red; the middle line is the border.
  • Many neurons together combine their small borders to draw the final curved boundary you see above.
